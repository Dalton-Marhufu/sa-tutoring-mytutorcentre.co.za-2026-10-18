Questions parents ask

The things you
actually want to know.

Still unsure? Phone the office on 011 782 3309 and ask a person.

Is MTTC a registered school?

Yes — MTTC is registered with the Gauteng Department of Education as an independent private school.

Which curriculum do you follow?

We follow the CAPS curriculum.

Can matriculants obtain a university entrance?

Yes. Our Grade 12 learners write the National Senior Certificate exam through the South African Comprehensive Assessment Institute (SACAI), which is accredited by Umalusi.

How big are the classes?

Classes average 8 to 10 learners, so every child gets personal attention.

Is there pressure on learners when it comes to dress?

No. Dress is not an issue — we cater for individuals.

What is the language of instruction?

All classes are tutored in English.
