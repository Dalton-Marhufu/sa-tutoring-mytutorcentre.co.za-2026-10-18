# Redesign Analysis

## Lead Details
- **Business Name:** My Tutor Centre (MTTC)
- **Domain:** mytutorcentre.co.za
- **Industry:** Tutoring / independent private school
- **Email:** info@mytutorcentre.co.za
- **Phone:** 011 782 3309 / 3678
- **City/Region:** Saxonwold, Rosebank, Johannesburg, Gauteng
- **Why this is a good lead:** A long-established, GDE-registered school with genuine testimonials and 17 years of history, but its website still runs a 2017 Divi theme that hides all of that credibility behind dense text pages.

## Score: 4/10
- Visual design and hierarchy: 0
- Clarity of offer and services: 1
- CTA strength and conversion flow: 1
- Trust signals and credibility: 1
- Mobile usability and general polish: 1

## Top 3 Issues
- The homepage opens with a dated 2017 slider and generic "ABOUT / WHAT WE OFFER" headings, so a parent cannot tell within five seconds what MTTC is, who it is for, or why it is different.
- The three actual offerings (full-time enrolment, part-time tutoring, one-to-one extra lessons) are buried in sub-pages, and the subject offering is presented as long unstyled grade lists rather than a scannable structure.
- Trust assets — GDE registration, CAPS curriculum, SACAI/Umalusi matric route, 8–10 learner classes, parent testimonials — exist but are scattered across separate pages with no badges, proof strip, or clear next step.

## Top 3 Improvements
- Lead with a plain-language promise and proof strip: "Professional tutoring from Grade 1 to 12", small classes of 8–10, GDE-registered, CAPS, NSC via SACAI.
- Restructure the offer into three clear paths (full-time, part-time, extra lessons) with a phase-based subject explorer so parents can find their child's grade in one click.
- Put credibility and conversion on one page: real parent quotes, the school-day timetable, an FAQ that answers registration and university-entrance questions, and a single "book a campus visit" CTA repeated throughout.
